FAQ

Questions before you begin?

Is the eye area safe?

Follow the included instructions carefully. Keep the device clean, use it only as directed, and stop using it if you experience discomfort.

Can I use it while pregnant or taking medication?

If you are pregnant, under medical care, or taking medication, consult your healthcare professional before using a light therapy device.

How long should each session be?

Follow the recommended session time provided with your LumaRestore mask. Do not use it for longer or more often than directed.

How often should I use it?

Use LumaRestore according to the included routine guidance. A consistent schedule is more useful than occasional, extended sessions.

When should I expect results?

Results and experiences vary from person to person. Focus on following the recommended routine consistently.
